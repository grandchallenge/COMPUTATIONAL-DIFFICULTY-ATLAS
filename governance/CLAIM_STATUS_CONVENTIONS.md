# Claim Status Conventions

Status: normative baseline

The monograph must expose epistemic status explicitly.

## Status vocabulary

- **THEOREM / PROVED:** established mathematical result under stated assumptions.
- **KNOWN CONTAINMENT:** established class inclusion.
- **KNOWN STRICT SEPARATION:** established non-equality / proper inclusion.
- **OPEN EQUALITY:** both containment directions needed for equality are not settled.
- **CONJECTURED SEPARATION:** widely or locally conjectured, but unproved.
- **MODEL-RELATIVE RESULT:** depends on oracle, circuit model, promise, reduction type, or other stated framework.
- **HEURISTIC / EMPIRICAL:** supported by experiments, benchmarks, or practice; not a theorem.
- **ENGINEERING OBSERVATION:** practical behavior of implementations or instance families.
- **PEDAGOGICAL SIMPLIFICATION:** intentionally incomplete local model, accompanied by a stated repair point.

## Figure rule

A figure may not use one visual style for both proved and conjectured relationships.

## Prose rule

Phrases such as "harder than," "efficient," "intractable," and "impossible" require the relevant formal or practical sense to be clear from context.

## Open-problem rule

Where a major unresolved equality is displayed, the surrounding text or caption must state that it is unresolved. Familiarity is not a substitute for explicit status.
